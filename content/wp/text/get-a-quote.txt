# Frequently Asked Questions
      Answers to common questions about car removal, scrap car pickup, and getting paid for your vehicle.
How much cash can I get for my car?
+
            The amount depends on your car’s make, model, age, condition, and current scrap metal value.
            We always aim to offer the best possible market price based on these factors.
Do you offer same-day car removal?
+
            Yes, in most cases we can arrange same-day pickup. Once you accept the quote, our team will
            schedule the earliest available time that suits you.
What types of vehicles do you buy?
+
            We buy all types of vehicles including old, damaged, scrap, unwanted, accident, and non-running cars,
            as well as vans, utes, and 4WDs.
Is towing and pickup really free?
+
            Yes, our towing service is completely free. There are no hidden fees — the quoted price is the amount you receive.
What documents do I need to sell my car?
+
            Typically, a valid photo ID and proof of ownership are required. Our team will guide you through any simple
            paperwork needed to complete the process legally.
How quickly will I get paid?
+
            Payment is made instantly once you accept the offer and before we take the vehicle. You can choose cash or bank transfer.
Do you remove cars that are not running?
+
            Absolutely. We specialise in removing non-running, damaged, and scrap vehicles using professional towing equipment.
What happens to my car after it’s collected?
+
            After pickup, the vehicle is taken to a licensed dismantling and recycling facility where usable parts are recovered
            and remaining materials are responsibly recycled.
